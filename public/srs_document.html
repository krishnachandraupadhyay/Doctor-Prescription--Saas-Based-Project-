1.0 Executive Summary & Vision

MediPortal Cloud is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant Medical Practice Management and Electronic Prescription SaaS platform. The software empowers outpatient clinics, polyclinics, nursing homes, and multi-specialty hospitals to streamline their clinical operations, digital prescription generation, OPD token queues, vitals tracking, staff administration, and billing.

The platform features an advanced Clinic-Centric Subscription Governance Engine that enforces strict plan-based feature provisioning, doctor quotas, staff capacities, and real-time session lockouts. Doctors and clinic staff inherit subscription rights automatically from their parent healthcare facility, providing clean organizational billing and security.

3.0 Overall Architecture & User Classes

The application is architected around a Role-Based Multi-Guard Authentication Matrix. Every actor operates within a strictly partitioned domain with automated subscription-aware middleware:

Actor / User Class Authentication Guard Primary Responsibilities & Capabilities
Super Administrator web (role: admin) Platform governance, SaaS plan creation, feature catalog editing, subscriber management, global metrics, clinic onboarding oversight.
Onboarding Manager web (role: onboarding) Clinic credential verification, physical verification checklist, doctor approval, compliance document inspection.
Clinic / Hospital Admin clinic Practice operations, medical team onboarding (up to plan quota), staff hiring (up to plan quota), revisit rule configuration, payment categories, prescription branding.
Consulting Doctor doctor OPD consultations, digital prescription authoring, formulary lookup, drug interaction alerts, patient history inspection, PDF prescription download.
Receptionist member (role: receptionist) Front desk patient registration, token generation, daily OPD queue ordering, revisit policy verification, fee receipt generation.
Clinical Staff / Nurse member (role: staff) Patient triage, physical examinations, vitals recording (Blood Pressure, Pulse, Temperature, SpO2, Blood Sugar).
Architectural Principle: Clinic-Held Tenant Model
Individual doctors and staff members do not purchase or maintain independent subscriptions. The Clinic/Hospital is the singular billable subscriber entity. When a Clinic's subscription plan is active, all doctors and staff mapped to that clinic inherit access rights seamlessly.

4.0 Clinic-Centric Multi-Tenant Subscription Architecture

The subscription engine enforces monetization, tiering, and operational capacities through four fundamental mechanisms:

  • Automatic Hierarchy Resolution: The system identifies the calling context (Clinic, Doctor, or Staff) and dynamically traces it back to the parent Clinic instance.
  • Ceiling Quotas (Hard Enforcement): Prevents resource allocation beyond the purchased tier (e.g. attempting to add a 3rd doctor when the plan allows 2).
  • Feature Flag Provisioning: Entire functional modules (e.g., Revisit Rules, Staff Accounts) are toggle-gated; unpurchased modules are hidden from UI and blocked at the route level.
  • Real-time Expiration Interceptors: Time-based validity checks run during login attempts and continuous session requests.

6.0 Feature Catalog & Modular Access Control

The platform defines 10 standardized, discrete feature identifiers that Super Admins can dynamically toggle for any subscription plan:

1. Prescription Writing & PDF Generation prescriptions

Enables doctor consultation desk, smart drug search, dosage calculator, advice snippets, and printable high-definition PDF prescriptions with QR code verification.

2. Patient Management & Medical History patients

Demographic record storage, past visit tracking, symptom archives, and multi-doctor patient history timeline.

3. Vitals & Physical Examination Tracking vitals_exam

Pre-consultation triage module allowing nursing staff to log Blood Pressure, Pulse Rate, Temperature, SpO2, and Random Blood Sugar.

4. Custom Medicine & Formulary Master medicines_master

Custom pharmacology catalog management allowing clinics to create proprietary brand names, generic formulations, default dosages, and intake instructions.

5. Staff & Receptionist Accounts staff_management

Dedicated role-separated logins for front desk receptionists and clinical support staff. Governed by max_staff quota ceiling.

6. Consultation Fees & Payment Categories payment_categories

Custom fee category creation (General OPD, Emergency, Specialist Consultation, Procedure Fees) and price configuration.

7. Patient Revisit Rules & Discounts revisit_rules

Automated policy engine that recognizes returning patients within a clinic-defined window (e.g., 7 days) and automatically waives or discounts consultation fees.

8. Clinic Branding & Prescription Customization custom_branding

Clinic letterhead upload, custom header/footer layout, doctor digital signature integration, clinic stamp, and custom prescription canvas design.

9. Clinic & Doctor Document Vault document_storage

Digital repository for clinic registration certificates, medical board licenses, and compliance documents.

10. Practice Performance & Analytics Reports analytics_reports

Aggregated visual reporting on daily OPD trends, doctor consultation volumes, fee collection totals, and patient retention rates.

7.0 Subscription-Based Login & Session Security

The security boundary guarantees that healthcare facilities cannot access the system after plan expiration. The enforcement operates on two distinct layers:

7.1 Pre-Authentication Gate (Login Controller)

When a user enters credentials on the central /login screen, the system executes the following strict verification sequence:

  1. Credential Validation: Hash verification via Bcrypt (cost 12).
  2. Account Status: Checks isdeleted == 0 and status == active.
  3. Verification Check: Confirms the clinic and doctor/staff accounts are officially verified.
  4. Subscription Interception:
    • For Clinic Admins: Executes $clinic->currentSubscription(). If expired, suspended, or missing, login is rejected with: "Your clinic's subscription expired on DD Mon YYYY. Please renew your plan to log in."
    • For Doctors: Traces the doctor's registered clinic. If the clinic subscription is inactive or expired, login is rejected with: "Access Denied: Your clinic's ({Clinic Name}) subscription expired on DD Mon YYYY. Doctor login is disabled until renewed."
    • For Receptionists & Staff: Traces parent clinic hierarchy. If the clinic subscription is inactive or expired, login is rejected with: "Access Denied: Your clinic's ({Clinic Name}) subscription expired on DD Mon YYYY. Staff login is disabled until renewed."

7.2 Active Session Lockout (Middleware)

For active sessions already open when a subscription reaches its midnight expiration time:

  • The CheckSubscriptionAccess (alias: subscription.active) middleware executes on every protected HTTP request.
  • If SubscriptionService::hasActiveSubscription() returns false, the middleware immediately invalidates the active session (Auth::logout()), clears CSRF tokens, and redirects the user to /login with an explicit expiration notice.

Req. ID Feature Name Detailed Requirement Specification
REQ-CLN-01 Doctor Management with Quota Ceiling Clinic Admin shall register consulting doctors. The system shall evaluate canAddDoctor() against the plan's max_doctors quota. If capacity is full, the Add button is disabled and submission blocked.
REQ-CLN-02 Staff Management with Quota Ceiling Clinic Admin shall manage Receptionists and Nurses. If plan feature staff_management is disabled, the module is hidden. If feature is enabled, capacity is capped at max_staff.

REQ-CLN-04 Prescription Design & Letterhead Clinic Admin shall upload logo, digital signature, header/footer assets. Gated behind custom_branding feature.
REQ-CLN-05 Doctor Correction Requests Clinic Admin shall review, approve, or reject profile correction requests submitted by doctors (qualification updates, license updates).

10.0 Entity Relationship Model Overview

The data model is structured around a central clinics table holding 1-to-N relationships with doctors, members (staff), patients, subscriptions, and payment_categories. Subscriptions bind to subscription_plans, which dynamically aggregate records from the subscription_features master catalog.
